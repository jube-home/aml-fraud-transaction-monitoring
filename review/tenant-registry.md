# Slice review: tenant-registry

Agent-mode review, 2026-11-08. Steps 0, 2, 3, 4, 5. Nothing was built and no test was run (owner's serial pass). The
foundation slices (shell-auth, wire-and-api, form-model-engine, data-grid) are inherited and not re-reviewed.

## 1. Summary and verdict

Verdict: READY AFTER OWNER ACTIONS, with two owner decisions. The landlord-only gate is sound on every verb (service
`EnsureLandlord`, 403 at the endpoint, page denial, menu hiding) and is already well covered by service and pen tests.
Real gaps were in tenant lifecycle: a Landlord tenant could be soft-deleted or deactivated through the API
(self-lockout), tenant names accepted padding, control characters and bidirectional overrides (look-alike or reordered
names in the switcher), and there was no service test for the optimistic-concurrency conflict. These are fixed, with 6
service test methods and 1 pen test added (all unrun, uncompiled). A wider design gap (tenant Active/Deleted is not
enforced against the tenant's own users) lives in shell-auth's file and is raised as a decision.

Before commit: (1) owner runs the manual test (section 9); (2) the serial pass builds and runs
`TenantRegistryServiceTests` (+6 methods) and `PenTestTenantRegistry` (+1); (3) owner decides finding 3; (4) owner
supplies screenshots and resolves docs TODOs.
